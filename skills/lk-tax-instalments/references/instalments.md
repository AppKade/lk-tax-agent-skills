# Instalment planning procedure

Run schema and read the instalments plan definition. Supported planning covers YA 2026/27 only and is separate from the YA 2025/26 annual estimate. Collect the scope answers for that current year, selected quarter and method eligibility independently. Do not reuse another year's facts by default.

Collect gross_tax_basis, explicit basis_source, preparation under the selected method and absence of foreign credits in the basis. An external basis is not verified by the helper. Explicit selection of session_annual_gross_tax is available only for Method 1 and must match the current annual case's gross tax, with no unresolved flags/anomalies. Matching arithmetic is not verification of a final return or legal method eligibility.

Collect actual prior instalments, APIT already deducted and expected remaining, and AIT/WHT already deducted and expected remaining. Reconcile without overlap, exclude final withholding/future payments and explicitly confirm zeros. First-quarter earlier instalments are zero only after confirmation. Distinguish actual remittance receipts from scheduled amounts.

The schema contains method-specific prior-taxable-income, prior-return/SET and revision facts. Confirm the required facts from records. A switch from standard needs the supported lower-income/quarter/notice conditions. Do not invent notice submission, a prior return, SET or revision history.

Use the calculator output for all arithmetic. Show its source-backed dates, administrative requirements, preview rounding caution and display_summary. The migrated payment-evidence research is historical and incomplete; it does not authorize bank fields, current payment codes or an actual payment. Verify current official procedures separately when asked and do not submit payment or a tax notice.
