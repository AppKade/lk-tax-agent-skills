# Personal tax routes and realistic traps

| Situation | Preparation route | Main checks |
|---|---|---|
| First-time primary employee | Annual taxable salary, APIT and other explicitly confirmed categories | Gross versus take-home, bonuses/benefits, EPF is not a tax credit, zero tax is not a filing decision |
| Changes jobs or has a second employer | Reconcile every annual/partial certificate before aggregating | Distinct employer versus replacement; no invented primary/secondary/Table 08 split |
| Salary plus bank interest | Separate gross interest, withholding and net credits for every source | Wrong years, duplicated letters, missing scans and replacement certificates |
| New local freelancer with invoices | Pause for externally prepared taxable net profit | Receipts, fees and equipment are not themselves a confirmed tax profit; do not calculate it in chat |
| Prepared local freelancer/sole trader | Confirm external taxable net profit and actual advance credits/instalments | Sales, accounting profit, drawings and deposits are different quantities |
| Overseas freelancer | Separately supplied net LKR aggregate plus required eligibility questions | Conversion and remittance do not follow from customer location; one-source/one-country credit limits |
| Employee with a side business | Confirm local salary and prepared business profit separately | Count each source once, reconcile credits without overlap |
| Company owner | Clarify personal salary/dividends versus company tax | Company profit and drawings are not individual net business profit; company tax is unsupported |
| Losses, partnership, terminal benefits or special residency | Stop and refer under published scope | Do not omit genuine income or change flags to obtain an answer |
| Returning user | Review restored case and genuine changes | Fresh consent in a new conversation, new computation and worksheet acknowledgement |

Use only the relevant missing questions. Ask at most three at a time. An unknown amount stays unknown; a hypothetical persona, sample figure or fixture must never become a real user's fact.
